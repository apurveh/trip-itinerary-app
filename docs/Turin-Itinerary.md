# Turin 8-Day Itinerary — 1–8 July

**Travelers:** Couple
**Base:** Airbnb near Superga (kitchen — cooking in most days)
**Style:** Mix of Turin city + day trips (Alps foothills, lakes, vineyards)
**Budget approach:** Cheapest sensible option (public transport + cook-at-home)

> All prices below are converted at **1 EUR ≈ 7.47 DKK** (rate as of May 2026). Rates fluctuate, so treat these as a planning estimate, not a quote. Prices are **per person** unless marked "(couple)".

---

## Day 1 — Wed 1 July: Arrival, settle in, easy first day

You land at Turin Airport (TRN/Caselle) at 10am. Cheapest path to your Airbnb near Superga:

- **SADEM airport bus** to Porta Nuova / Porta Susa — ~56 DKK pp (~50 min, runs every 15–30 min)
- Then **bus 61 or 68** toward the Sassi area — ~13 DKK pp
- **Total airport → Airbnb for two:** ~120 DKK

**The day:**
- Around 1pm: drop bags, hit the closest **Carrefour or Pam supermarket** to stock the kitchen — pasta, eggs, prosciutto, bread, fruit, coffee, wine. First shop ~**300–450 DKK (couple)**
- Light lunch at home from groceries
- Afternoon: gentle stroll around the neighborhood + nap if you need it after the flight
- Evening: tram/bus into the city, walk **Piazza Castello → Piazza San Carlo → Via Roma** (the elegant arcaded streets) — no museum agenda, just orientation
- Dinner: cook at home

⚠️ **Heads up for Day 2:** Royal Palace is closed Wednesdays — but you'll be there Thursday, so no issue.

---

## Day 2 — Thu 2 July: Egyptian Museum + Royal Quarter

- **Morning: Museo Egizio** — ~135 DKK pp. Book online to skip the line. Second-largest Egyptian collection in the world after Cairo. Plan 2.5–3 hours
- Lunch at home (10-min tram back) OR a cheap *tavola calda* near the museum (~75–110 DKK pp)
- **Afternoon: Palazzo Reale + Royal Museums** complex — Armoury, Galleria Sabauda, gardens. One ticket covers it all (~110 DKK pp). Plan 2–3 hours
- **Aperitivo:** Turin invented the aperitivo. Hit a bar in **Piazza San Carlo or Piazza Vittorio** — ~60–75 DKK pp gets you a Vermouth or Negroni with generous snacks
- **Dinner:** cook at home

---

## Day 3 — Fri 3 July: Mole Antonelliana + Quadrilatero food crawl

- **Morning: Museo Nazionale del Cinema** inside the Mole Antonelliana — ~170 DKK pp for the combined ticket (museum + panoramic glass elevator). Buy online in advance
- **Lunch:** cook at home, OR grab a *panino* at a local salumeria (~45 DKK pp)
- **Afternoon:** explore the **Quadrilatero Romano** — Turin's oldest neighborhood, narrow medieval lanes, bookshops, gelato. If you arrive before 2pm, hit **Porta Palazzo market** (largest open-air market in Europe — great for cheese, bread, produce for tonight's dinner)
- **Evening:** cook a Piedmontese-style pasta with what you bought at the market

---

## Day 4 — Sat 4 July: Day trip — Sacra di San Michele + Avigliana lakes

This is the abbey that inspired Umberto Eco's *The Name of the Rose*.

- **Train:** Porta Nuova → Avigliana (~35 min, ~26 DKK each way pp)
- From Avigliana: taxi to Sant'Ambrogio (~110 DKK couple) and hike the **Mortera trail** up to the Sacra (~1.5 hr uphill, beautiful) — OR a local bus
- **Sacra entry:** ~60 DKK pp
- **Lunch:** pack sandwiches from your kitchen and eat with the view (saves ~150 DKK pp)
- **Afternoon:** back down to **Lacs d'Avigliana** for a relaxed walk by the water
- Back in Turin by evening, dinner at home

**Day total transit (couple):** ~260 DKK

---

## Day 5 — Sun 5 July: Superga day (your neighborhood!)

You're staying near Superga — make it count.

- Late morning: walk or bus to **Sassi station**, take the historic **Sassi–Superga rack tramway** up to the Basilica
- **Sunday return ticket: ~67 DKK pp** (Sat–Sun summer hours 9am–8pm; closed Wednesdays — fortunately you're going Sunday)
- Visit the **Basilica + Royal Tombs of the House of Savoy + the dome** for panoramic views of the Alps (~75 DKK pp combined)
- **Picnic lunch at the top** — pack from home. Benches with the best view in Turin
- **Afternoon:** hike down via the **Strada dei Colli** path back toward Sassi (~1 hr downhill, gorgeous)
- Sunday dinner at home

> 💡 Tip: many state museums (Royal Palace included) offer **free entry on the first Sunday of the month**. July's first Sunday is the 5th — check on the day if it's running, you might bag a free Royal Palace visit if you didn't already do Day 2 (or revisit). The Egyptian Museum is private and not included in this scheme.

## Day 6 — Mon 6 July: Langhe wine country day trip (Alba + La Morra)

The big one. Two options:

### Option A — Public transport (cheapest, ~150 DKK pp)
- **Train:** Porta Nuova → Alba (~1h15, change at Bra), ~75 DKK each way pp
- Walk Alba's truffle-and-hazelnut old town
- **Bus to La Morra** runs limited times — check ATL Cuneo timetables in advance

### Option B — 1-day economy car rental (~450–600 DKK couple)
- Rental from Porta Nuova area (Europcar/Hertz, book early). Add ~60–100 DKK fuel, ~50 DKK parking
- Drive the **Barolo wine road**: La Morra → Barolo → Monforte d'Alba — postcard-pretty hilltop villages
- Free / low-cost tastings at **Cantina Comunale di La Morra** (rated 4.7); buying a bottle or two ~110–225 DKK each
- Far more flexibility — recommended if budget allows

**Lunch:** pack a picnic to keep costs down. Dinner back at home.

---

## Day 7 — Tue 7 July: Reggia di Venaria (UNESCO Savoy palace)

The "Versailles of Italy" — open Tuesdays (closed Mondays).

- **Venaria Express bus** from Piazza Castello — ~22 DKK each way pp, drops at the palace
- **Combined ticket palace + gardens: ~150 DKK pp**. Plan 4–5 hours total: ~2h palace, ~2h gardens (they're enormous)
- **Lunch:** pack from home, or eat at the on-site café (~110–150 DKK pp)
- Back in Turin, evening: walk along the **Po river + Parco del Valentino** at sunset, see the Borgo Medievale (free)
- **Dinner at home** — start using up groceries before departure

---

## Day 8 — Wed 8 July: Departure day, light morning

Depending on flight time:

- If afternoon flight: morning at **Eataly Lingotto** (the original Eataly, flagship for Slow Food). Pick up wine, taggiasca olives, hazelnut spread for gifts
- Or quick stop at **Caffè Al Bicerin** for the famous Turin chocolate-coffee-cream drink (~75 DKK)
- **SADEM bus back to airport** ~56 DKK pp, or train + connection

## Practical info & money-saving notes

### Transit
- **GTT 7-day pass: ~135 DKK pp** for unlimited Turin city buses, trams, metro — pays off after Day 3
- Regional trains for day trips are separate and cheap (~25–80 DKK each way)

### Pass to consider
- **Torino+Piemonte Card**: ~250 DKK (3 days) / ~320 DKK (5 days) pp
- Covers Royal Palace, Egyptian Museum, Mole, Reggia di Venaria, AND discounts the Superga rack tram
- Do the math: if you visit 4+ paid sites, it pays off

### Cooking-in tips
- **Porta Palazzo market** Saturday morning: Turin's freshest and cheapest produce, cheese, meat
- One Piedmontese specialty to cook at home: *tajarin with butter and sage* — 30 minutes, magic
- *Vitello tonnato* — pre-made at any deli, no cooking needed, very Piedmontese
- Budget ~200–300 DKK/day for groceries (couple) cooking ~2 meals at home

### Weather (early July)
- Hot: 28–32°C / 82–90°F, often humid
- Plan museum days for hottest afternoons; outdoor stuff for morning/evening
- Bring a refillable water bottle — Turin's *toret* fountains are everywhere and free
